We already know your story

You don't have to explain it. Here it is:

Your crypto is locked in an exchange. Early on, you took a withdrawal and it worked — something nominal, $100, maybe $1,000. That's what convinced you it was real. So you put in more. Maybe everything. Then you tried to take out a real number — $50,000, $100,000 — and suddenly it wouldn't move. Now the exchange wants fees to release it: service fees, prepaid taxes, legal fees, transaction fees. And here's the part that keeps you up at night — they won't take the fees out of your balance. They want the money from your pocket.

If that sounds familiar, it's because it's a script. The small withdrawal, the big freeze, the fees from your pocket — every beat of it is the script, and you're inside it right now. An exchange that won't pay you out of your own balance isn't an exchange. Stop paying. Every fee you send disappears into the same hole as the rest.

When the exchange does that — this is where you go. The PFLASH shows you where your money actually is, which is the one thing they never want you to see.

Every wallet your money touched, traced across chains and mixers. The exchanges where funds landed — that matters, because exchanges know who owns their accounts ("subpoena" just means a court order making them say so). Exhibits, timelines, and a filing-ready package.
