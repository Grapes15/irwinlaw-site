The short version

Dan Irwin runs Irwin Law Office PLC, based in Dundas, Minnesota. The practice is deliberately narrow: divorce and family law for southern Minnesota, and a small number of select crypto fraud matters.

The family-law side is the traditional craft: divorce, custody, parenting time, asset division. On the crypto side: if you've been scammed, the path is to report it to law enforcement, PFLASH your crypto at phantomflash.com — a separate crypto-tracing service Dan founded — and then address your situation with the report in hand, with any lawyer you choose. Phantom Flash is not the law office, and using it does not create an attorney-client relationship. Irwin Law takes on only a small number of exceptional crypto matters, at its discretion.

The operating style is the same in both rooms: direct answers, honest odds, closed loops. If your case is weak, you hear it. If it's strong, you get a plan and closed loops.
