What I handle

Divorce

Minnesota is a no-fault state — nobody has to prove wrongdoing to end a marriage. Contested or uncontested, I handle filing, service, disclosures, negotiation, mediation, and trial across southern Minnesota.

Custody & Parenting Time

Minnesota courts decide custody on the child's best interests under Minn. Stat. § 518.17. I build the record that shows who you actually are as a parent — and I don't let the other side define you.

Asset Division

Minnesota divides marital property equitably — which is not the same as equally. Houses, retirement accounts, businesses, farms, and yes, cryptocurrency. If your spouse holds digital assets, you're in the one family-law office in Minnesota that can trace them.

Modifications & Enforcement

Life changes after the decree. Jobs end, people move, kids grow. When an existing order no longer fits — or the other side ignores it — I bring it back to court.
